📤 Step 1: Download Link Kaise Banayein (3 Best Free Ways)

Option A: Google Drive (Sabse Trusted Aur Professional)

  1. Apne browser mein drive.google.com kholein.
  2. Desktop se shoe-shop-pos.zip ko drag karke Google Drive mein chhor dein (Upload ho jayegi).
  3. File par Right-Click karein -> Share par click karein.
  4. General access mein "Anyone with the link" select karein.
  5. "Copy link" par click karein. Yeh link aapke paas copy ho gaya!

Option B: WeTransfer (Bina Login Ya Account Ke)

  1. wetransfer.com kholein.
  2. shoe-shop-pos.zip attach karein.
  3. 3 dots par click karke "Get transfer link" choose karein aur "Get a link" dabayein.
  4. 10 second mein direct download link ban jayega!
